Information you provide

You choose the display name, bio and optional photo on your profile. Story and chapter submissions appear publicly when you publish them.

Reading and writing activity

Your library and saved reading position help you return to a story. Draft chapters stay in the writer studio until you choose to publish.

Your choices and account controls

You can update your profile and notification preferences in Settings. Account actions are grouped under Privacy and security.
