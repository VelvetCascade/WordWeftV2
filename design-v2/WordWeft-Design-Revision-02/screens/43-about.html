About WordWeft

A place for stories
and the people behind them.

WordWeft is a reading and writing platform made around the simple act of sharing a story.

A good place to read.

Discover stories, poems and essays. Save a place, leave a thought in the margin, and return when the next chapter arrives.

A clear place to write.

Start with a draft. Build your chapters at your own pace. When you are ready, share them with readers and keep ownership of what you create.

At the beginning

WordWeft is still growing. If you want to help shape it, join the founding writers program or share what would make reading and writing here better.
